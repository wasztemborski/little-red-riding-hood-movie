# Flower Meadow

**Summary**: The clearing full of flowers where [[red-riding-hood]] makes the [[leave-the-path]] decision and first meets [[wolf]].

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

On the forest path the girl spots a meadow full of flowers (polana pełna kwiatów) and remembers that her [[grandmother]] loves flowers and will enjoy a bouquet (source: story.md).

She steps off the path toward the meadow and begins gathering blooms (source: story.md). The further she walks, the more beautiful the flowers become, and she does not notice how far she has strayed from both the road and the meadow itself (source: story.md).

Only when the bouquet is finished does she turn back — and [[wolf]] stands before her (source: story.md).
