# Forest

**Summary**: The deep woods where [[grandmother]]'s [[grandmother-cottage]] stands and where [[wolf]] intercepts [[red-riding-hood]].

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

The story opens "long ago at the edge of the forest" (dawno dawno temu na skraju lasu), where [[red-riding-hood]] lives with her family (source: story.md).

A forest path leads to the [[grandmother-cottage]], which sits in the middle of the forest (source: story.md). It is within the forest — past the [[flower-meadow]] — that the girl wanders off the road and meets [[wolf]] (source: story.md).

The [[woodcutter]] escorts Red Riding Hood home through the forest after the rescue (source: story.md).
