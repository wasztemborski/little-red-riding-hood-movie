# Grandmother Cottage

**Summary**: The house in the middle of the [[forest]] where [[grandmother]] lies ill and where [[wolf]] swallows both her and [[red-riding-hood]].

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

The cottage stands in the middle of the [[forest]] and [[wolf]] finds it without trouble after learning the location from [[red-riding-hood]] (source: story.md).

He knocks and enters before hearing an answer, swallows the bedridden grandmother, and dons her cap and glasses to wait under the quilt (source: story.md). [[red-riding-hood]] follows and is swallowed as well (source: story.md).

[[woodcutter]] notices the cottage's door ajar and loud snoring, peers through the window, and intervenes (source: story.md).
