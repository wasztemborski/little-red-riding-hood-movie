# Leave the Path

**Summary**: [[red-riding-hood]]'s decision to abandon her mother's instruction and step off the road to pick flowers for [[grandmother]] — the choice that puts her in [[wolf]]'s way.

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

**Context**: [[mother]] has explicitly told the girl to go straight to grandmother's and not to stray from the road (source: story.md).

**Decision**: Seeing the [[flower-meadow]], the girl reasons: "Mother warned me to go straight to grandmother's and not stray from the path. But nothing will happen if I stop here a moment to pick flowers" (source: story.md). She steps off the path toward the meadow.

**Rationale**: Her grandmother loves flowers and would be delighted by a bouquet (source: story.md); the risk of a short detour seems negligible.

**Consequence**: The flowers draw her further and further until she has wandered away from both the road and the meadow; when she finally turns back, [[wolf]] is standing there (source: story.md). The episode ends with the moral that staying on the designated road avoids many troubles (source: story.md).
