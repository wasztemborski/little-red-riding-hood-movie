# Mother

**Summary**: Red Riding Hood's mother, who sends her daughter to the sick [[grandmother]] with medicine and warns her to keep to the path.

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

The mother sends [[red-riding-hood]] to her sick [[grandmother]] in the middle of the [[forest]], packing a basket with medicine and treats (source: story.md).

Her explicit instruction is to go straight to grandmother's and not to stray from the road (source: story.md). This warning is exactly what makes the girl's [[leave-the-path]] decision a transgression rather than a casual detour (source: story.md).
