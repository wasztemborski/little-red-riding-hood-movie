# Grandmother

**Summary**: Red Riding Hood's sick grandmother, living alone in the middle of the [[forest]]; the first victim of [[wolf]].

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

The grandmother is ill in bed, and [[mother]] sends [[red-riding-hood]] to her with medicine and sweets (source: story.md). She lives in the [[grandmother-cottage]] in the middle of the [[forest]] and loves flowers, which is what tempts Red Riding Hood to [[leave-the-path]] (source: story.md).

She loves flowers so much that the girl is sure a bouquet will please her (source: story.md).

[[wolf]] reaches her cottage first, knocks, enters before she can answer, and swallows her while she lies in bed (source: story.md). She is later released alive from the wolf's belly together with her granddaughter after [[woodcutter]] kills the beast (source: story.md).
