# Wolf

**Summary**: The antagonist who tricks [[red-riding-hood]], eats [[grandmother]], impersonates her, and is dispatched by [[woodcutter]].

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

The wolf politely accosts Red Riding Hood on the [[flower-meadow]], winning her trust at once (source: story.md). feigning sympathy for the grandmother's illness, he smiles slyly and vanishes after learning the grandmother's location from her (source: story.md).

He reaches the [[grandmother-cottage]] in the middle of the [[forest]] before her, enters without waiting for an answer, and swallows the grandmother whole (source: story.md). He then puts on her cap and glasses and slips under the quilt to impersonate her (source: story.md).

When [[red-riding-hood]] arrives he answers her three questions — big eyes "to see you better", big ears "to hear you better", and sharp teeth "to eat you" — then jumps out of bed and swallows her too, returning under the quilt to sleep (source: story.md).

His loud snoring gives him away to [[woodcutter]], who sees the wolf sleeping in the bed through the window and finishes him off (source: story.md). The grandmother and Red Riding Hood jump out of his belly alive and unharmed (source: story.md).
