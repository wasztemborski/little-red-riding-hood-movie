# Red Riding Hood

**Summary**: The title character — a little girl in a red cap who is sent to bring medicine to her sick grandmother and strays from the path.

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

Known in the source as Czerwony Kapturek, named for the red cap she always wears (source: story.md). Her mother sends her to her sick grandmother in the middle of the [[forest]], carrying a basket with medicine and treats (source: story.md).

Warned to stay on the path, she instead makes the pivotal [[leave-the-path]] decision to pick flowers on a [[flower-meadow]], wandering far from both the meadow and the road (source: story.md). There she meets [[wolf]], to whom she reveals exactly where [[grandmother]] lives (source: story.md).

At the cottage she questions the wolf-in-disguise about his big eyes, ears and sharp teeth, and is swallowed along with her grandmother (source: story.md). Both are rescued alive by [[woodcutter]], and she is escorted home (source: story.md).

Afterwards she never strays from the path again, having learned that staying on the designated road avoids many troubles (source: story.md).
