# Woodcutter

**Summary**: The hero who passes the [[grandmother-cottage]], notices [[wolf]] sleeping inside, and rescues [[red-riding-hood]] and her grandmother.

**Sources**: `00-inbox/story.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

The gajowy (forester/woodcutter) happens to be passing by the [[grandmother-cottage]] (source: story.md). From a distance he sees the door ajar and hears loud snoring, wondering whether it is the grandmother snoring (source: story.md).

Curious, he looks through the window and sees the wolf sleeping soundly in the bed; guessing what has happened, he enters and dispatches the beast in no time (source: story.md). [[grandmother]] and [[red-riding-hood]] spring from the wolf's belly alive and healthy (source: story.md).

They embrace and thank him for saving their lives, and he walks the girl home through the [[forest]] (source: story.md).
