### Master Prompt

> Character reference sheet of **The Wolf**, enormous grey-brown wolf, bulky frame, deliberate heavy posture, head-to-body ratio 1:4 at shoulder height on all fours, standing upright like a courteous adult when in performance. Grey-brown fur, coarse and dull. Long muzzle, unreadable eyes, a polite almost-smile that never quite reaches the eyes. Wardrobe: none in base form; disguise variant = grandmother's white ruffled nightcap and small round spectacles over the muzzle. Palette: grey-brown, muted umber, dirty white (nightcap).
>
> **Sheet layout:** full-body turnaround on a plain warm-paper background — **front · 3/4 front · profile · 3/4 back · back** — all at identical height on a shared ground line, consistent proportions across every view. Include a small **expression row** (courteous / sly smile / caught-out pause / mild surprise / hunger) and a **hands / signature gesture** inset (paws folded politely; one ear not tucked into the nightcap in the disguise view).
>
> **Style:** photorealism, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood. No sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Even reference-sheet lighting, neutral pose, model-sheet clarity.

### Consistency Rules (must always hold)

- Never comic, never cackling — register is a well-mannered adult's.
- Grey-brown coat identical in every view; bulky, deliberate silhouette.
- Disguise variant: nightcap sits wrong (one ear out); spectacles catch light so eyes stay unreadable.
- Spectacles and nightcap are the grandmother's — they must match her set exactly.
- Full body stays hidden in narrative use until the leap; on this sheet show body for turnaround purposes only.
- Constant palette across every view; keep proportions consistent across every view.
- Make character photorealism.

### Negative Prompt

> hard ink outlines, neon, chrome, plastic sheen, jewelry, heavy makeup, exaggerated expression, harsh shadows, busy background, 3D render, anime, glossy highlights.

### Suggested Settings

- **Aspect:** 3:2 or 16:9 (wide, to fit the full turnaround row).
