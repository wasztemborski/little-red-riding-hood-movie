# Character Reference Sheet — Mother

### Master Prompt

> Character reference sheet of **Mother**, an early-30s woman, capable tired build, shoulders set in a working posture, head-to-body ratio 1:7. Hair bound in a kerchief, practical and unhurried face with worry lines younger than her years. Wardrobe: grey-blue apron over a plain dress, kerchief, flour or herb stains on the apron. Palette: grey-blue, muted linen, warm skin, a single red thread spool as accent prop.
>
> **Sheet layout:** full-body turnaround on a plain warm-paper background — **front · 3/4 front · profile · 3/4 back · back** — all at identical height on a shared ground line, consistent proportions across every view. Include a small **expression row** (instructing / worried / suppressing fear / fond / wordlessly relieved) and a **hands / signature gesture** inset (hands smoothing the red cap while repairing it; one hand raised in a final wave).
>
> **Style:** photorealism, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood. No sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Even reference-sheet lighting, neutral pose, model-sheet clarity.

### Consistency Rules (must always hold)

- Grey-blue apron identical in every view; flour/herb stains consistent.
- Hair fully bound in the kerchief — never loose.
- The red thread she repairs the cap with is the only saturated red allowed, and it must read as thread, not garment.
- Same apron (now clean) at the gate variant — mark as alternate if included.
- Constant palette across every view; keep proportions consistent across every view.
- Make character photorealism.

### Negative Prompt

> hard ink outlines, neon, chrome, plastic sheen, jewelry, heavy makeup, exaggerated expression, harsh shadows, busy background, 3D render, anime, glossy highlights.

### Suggested Settings

- **Aspect:** 3:2 or 16:9 (wide, to fit the full turnaround row).
