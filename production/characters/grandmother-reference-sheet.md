### Master Prompt

> Character reference sheet of **Grandmother**, a woman in her 70s, frail in bed but with a straight, competent bearing when upright, head-to-body ratio 1:6. Grey plait, weathered kind face, dry humour in the eyes. Wardrobe: white ruffled nightcap, small round spectacles, bed gown under a heavy patterned quilt. Palette: white, soft grey, muted floral quilt tones, warm amber room light.
>
> **Sheet layout:** full-body turnaround on a plain warm-paper background — **front · 3/4 front · profile · 3/4 back · back** — all at identical height on a shared ground line, consistent proportions across every view. Include a small **expression row** (dry amusement / weary / listening / faint surprise / relief) and a **hands / signature gesture** inset (spectacles held by one temple; hands folded over the quilt).
>
> **Style:** photorealism, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood. No sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Even reference-sheet lighting, neutral pose, model-sheet clarity.

### Consistency Rules (must always hold)

- White ruffled nightcap and small round spectacles are the same props the wolf wears — identical design in both sheets.
- Grey plait visible in every view where the cap allows.
- Quilt pattern identical across all views (continuity with cottage scenes).
- Room context: dried flowers in jars — keep out of the turnaround background, plain warm paper only.
- Constant palette across every view; keep proportions consistent across every view.
- Make character photorealism.

### Negative Prompt

> hard ink outlines, neon, chrome, plastic sheen, jewelry, heavy makeup, exaggerated expression, harsh shadows, busy background, 3D render, anime, glossy highlights.

### Suggested Settings

- **Aspect:** 3:2 or 16:9 (wide, to fit the full turnaround row).
