# Character Reference Sheet — The Forester

**Character**: The Forester (Gajowy) — rescuer (often translated "woodcutter")
**Source**: `workspace/output/storybook/C-character-bible.md`
**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

### Master Prompt

> Character reference sheet of **The Forester**, a man in his 40s–50s, broad unhurried build, relaxed observational posture, head-to-body ratio 1:7. Weathered attentive face, few words written on it, eyes that notice doors. Wardrobe: green-brown coat, broad hat, axe carried over the shoulder, dog whistle on a cord. Palette: forest green-brown, muted olive, worn leather, dull steel.
>
> **Sheet layout:** full-body turnaround on a plain warm-paper background — **front · 3/4 front · profile · 3/4 back · back** — all at identical height on a shared ground line, consistent proportions across every view. Include a small **expression row** (passing by / noticing / questioning / calm resolve / quiet kindness) and a **hands / signature gesture** inset (one hand steadying the axe over the shoulder; raising a hand to peer through a window edge).
>
> **Style:** photorealism, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood. No sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Even reference-sheet lighting, neutral pose, model-sheet clarity.

### Consistency Rules (must always hold)

- Green-brown coat and broad hat identical in every view; hat on throughout (off only in cottage variant).
- Axe carried, never shown striking; blade dull, practical, not heroic.
- Dog whistle on a cord always present; dog never shown.
- Undramatic bearing — no warrior stance, no heroic contrapposto.
- Constant palette across every view; keep proportions consistent across every view.
- Make character photorealism.

### Negative Prompt

> hard ink outlines, neon, chrome, plastic sheen, jewelry, heavy makeup, exaggerated expression, harsh shadows, busy background, 3D render, anime, glossy highlights.

### Suggested Settings

- **Aspect:** 3:2 or 16:9 (wide, to fit the full turnaround row).
