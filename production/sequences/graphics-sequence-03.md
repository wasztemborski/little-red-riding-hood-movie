# Part III — The Cottage: Storybook Illustration Prompts

Storybook illustration prompts, one per scene. Each prompt is self-contained and carries the full style so it can be pasted directly into an image generator.

**Global style suffix**: *hand-painted watercolor and gouache, Scandinavian storybook illustration, pastel palette, soft diffuse light, rounded organic shapes, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood, no photorealism, no sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Aspect 16:9.*

**Context for Part III**: The cottage interior is dim—light enters only through the front window and open door, which means anyone in the bed is backlit and hard to read. This is why the girl cannot tell. Jars of dried flowers line every shelf. The wolf in disguise wears grandmother's white ruffled nightcap and small round spectacles, absurd and horrifying at once. Never show his full body between the conversation and the leap. The spectacles catch the light so the eyes are not readable until the questions begin. Sustain dread throughout—the cottage should feel like a trap even as it appears ordinary.
