# Part II — Off the Path: Storybook Illustration Prompts

Storybook illustration prompts, one per scene. Each prompt is self-contained and carries the full style so it can be pasted directly into an image generator.

**Global style suffix**: *hand-painted watercolor and gouache, Scandinavian storybook illustration, pastel palette, soft diffuse light, rounded organic shapes, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood, no photorealism, no sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Aspect 16:9.*

**Context for Part II**: The forest transitions from familiar woodland (soft birch, friendly light) through deeper, older trees (tall, columnar, quieter) to the enchanted meadow (blown-out warm light, haze, saturated pastels, the only place that looks like a painting). The pale ribbon of the forest path is the visual spine—always visible until it vanishes. The meadow has no clear far edge; it thins into trees so gradually that neither girl nor audience can say where it ends. This is temptation made beautiful.

## S016 — He Is Simply There

> The girl turns on her heel—and the wolf is standing in front of her. She did not see him come. He is simply there, as if he had always been there, waiting. He is enormous, grey-brown, but his face is calm and polite. He smiles faintly. The meadow light catches in his eyes. The girl's arms are still full of flowers. Her face goes from fear to surprise to relief—perhaps this creature can help her. **Part II Summary**: The path is lost; the rule is broken. The meadow's beauty is real, but it becomes a trap through the girl's own desire to please. She reasons herself off the path and into the wolf's waiting. Her greatest strength—her openness, her willingness to talk, her desire to be good—becomes her greatest vulnerability.
