# Part I — The Errand: Storybook Illustration Prompts

Storybook illustration prompts, one per scene. Each prompt is self-contained and carries the full style so it can be pasted directly into an image generator.

**Global style suffix**: *hand-painted watercolor and gouache, Scandinavian storybook illustration, pastel palette, soft diffuse light, rounded organic shapes, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood, no photorealism, no sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. Aspect 16:9.*

**Context for Part I**: Warm dawn light filtering through cottage windows and forest edges. Ochre and yellow timber, weathered wood textures. The red cap is the only saturated colour—a visual anchor throughout. Soft shadows, gentle morning air. Characters speak volumes through gesture and expression; maintain the quiet intimacy of a child's world at its threshold.

## S001 — Cold Open: A Spot of Red

## S002 — The Cap

> Inside a small, cluttered cottage on the forest's edge, a girl of about eight years old sits still while her mother carefully ties the knot of her red woollen cap under her chin. Sunlight streams through small-paned windows across worn wood furniture and handmade objects. The cap is positioned just so—a ritual moment. The girl's expression is solemn and watchful; the mother's hands are gentle but determined. ## S004 — Packing

> A wicker basket sits open on a wooden table. The mother carefully places items into it—a cloth, jars of medicine, bread, treats. Her hands move with care and love. The girl watches from nearby, sometimes helping to fold the cloth. Afternoon light slants across the table. The basket grows fuller with each moment; this errand is serious, purposeful, made of small acts of devotion. Warm, domestic, the texture of worn linen and woven willow. *hand-painted watercolor and gouache, Scandinavian storybook illustration, pastel palette, soft diffuse light, rounded organic shapes, delicate paper texture, gentle depth, melancholic and warm mood, no photorealism, no sharp contours, no neon, no chrome, no plastic. ## S005 — The Rule

> The mother kneels down beside her daughter, taking both her small hands in hers. Her face is serious and loving; she speaks the rule plainly: stay on the path. ## S006 — The Gate

> A low wooden gate at the edge of the cottage property swings slowly open. The girl stands in the doorway, basket in hand, red cap tied firm. Her mother stands behind in the warm cottage doorway. Beyond the gate, a strip of open grass leads to the treeline—an abrupt, dark green wall that rises up like a living boundary. The girl takes her first step toward it. The gate will swing shut behind her; the forest waits. **Part I Summary**: This sequence establishes home, identity, love, and the boundary between safety and the wild. The red cap transforms from costume to symbol. The rule is given and accepted. The child is ready and willing—and that willingness, combined with her goodness, will be her undoing.
