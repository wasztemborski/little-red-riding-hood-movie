**Context for Part IV**: Late afternoon moving toward evening. The forest becomes simply a wood again—the change is in the girl, not in it. Soft golden light, lengthening shadows. The cottage is restored and candle-lit; there is warmth in small acts of care—giving medicine, placing flowers in water. The meadow is seen from the path at dusk, still beautiful, but not entered. The final image shows the girl walking freely on the path beside her rescuer, the red cap secure, the rule now chosen rather than imposed. Light is warm, hopeful, quietly wiser.

**Part IV Summary**: Rescue through ordinary attentiveness. The forester notices, acts, and saves. The girl completes her errand and is restored. The meadow is still beautiful, but she walks past it freely, having learned—not through punishment, but through lived consequence—that some rules exist to protect, and that breaking them has weight. The final image mirrors the opening: a spot of red in the grey forest, but now moving with purpose and choice.
