## Source availability statement

The entire source text was available and analysed. It is a short illustrated-book
retelling, not a novel: approximately 700 words across 8 narrative paragraphs.
Nothing is missing. Because the source is this short, the adaptation necessarily
**expands** rather than condenses, and every expansion is flagged in
[Document F](F-source-to-screen-mapping.md) as *Visual interpretation* or
*Proposed change*.

Note on the source's variant: this text is labelled "według Charlesa Perraulta"
(after Perrault) but follows the **Grimm ending** — a `gajowy` (forester /
woodcutter) rescues both victims alive from the wolf's belly. Perrault's own
ending has no rescue. The storybook follows the supplied text, not Perrault.
